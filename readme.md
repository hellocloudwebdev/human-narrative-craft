# Verdict

## Fair — **9.7 / 10**

Fair succeeds through nuanced character psychology, subtle moral complexity, and reflective storytelling. It invites readers to interpret difficult emotions without forcing a single conclusion, giving the narrative lasting impact beyond its final page.

---

## Rosa — **9.6 / 10**

Rosa excels through immersive scene construction, grounded realism, and consistently strong pacing. It transforms an ordinary emergency into an emotionally compelling story through careful observation and understated writing.
